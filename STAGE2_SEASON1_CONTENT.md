## Season 1 Core Statement
Season 1 is about becoming a player identity inside a small school club, where:
- `Training` expresses **who you are** (effort + style).
- Mainline matches express **how you hold pressure** (process + outcome).
- Relationships shift mostly through **table behavior**, not speeches.
- The emotional endpoint is: the club stops being “a room of strangers” and becomes *a group*.

## Macro Shape (Planning Target)
- Nominal timescale: approximately `8 weeks` of school life (see `STORY_GROUNDING_BIBLE.md`) — a planning index, not a hard constraint.
- Act scaffold:
  - **Act 1 (Weeks 1–2):** Entry + Belonging
  - **Act 2 (Weeks 3–6):** Friction + Identity
  - **Act 3 (Weeks 7–8):** Respect + Continuation
- The “5-phase” cadence from earlier notes is treated as *tone*, nested inside the 3-act scaffold:
  - Entry → Belonging → Fracture → Pressure → Tournament/Aftermath

  - Placement produces the first stable “you’re in / you’re seen” state.

### Anchor B — Mid-Arc Conflict Resolution Match (Inflection)
- This anchor exists to satisfy the core story rule: *problems are solved on the table*.
- Fixed match slot:
  - Week `6` official match: club tournament semifinal — **major-rival rematch** (mainline)
- Pre-match must select **one** conflict lens (tonal divergence, not structural branching):
  - `low_training` → accountability/commitment conflict (Jolo-led per `STORY_ANCHOR_BEAT_MATRIX.md`)
  - `high_training` → ambition/philosophy conflict (often Tix-led)
  - `style_mismatch` → “right way to play” disagreement (Kai/Aya as stabilizers)
- Optional insert allowed (deterministic) before the Week `6` semifinal:
  - `inserted_personal_match` (see `STORY_REACTIVITY_MODEL.md`)

### Anchor C — Regional Decider + Quiet Aftermath
- Final pressure beat: earned expression under real stakes.
- Fixed match slot:
  - Week `8` official match: regional decider (mainline)
- Aftermath is quiet and grounded:
  - Continuity ending (“practice again tomorrow” energy), not trophy spectacle.
- Variant shading (from `STORY_ANCHOR_BEAT_MATRIX.md`):
  - `surge_path` / `fade_path` / `rebound_path`
  - Win/loss changes *shade*, not validity.

## Mainline Week Spine (Resolved)
This schedule is the convergent spine. Each week has one mainline `Next Match` plus optional `Training` and deterministic inserts.

### Opponent Authoring (Resolved)
- Rivals/opponents are authored **per match** (no hidden progression).
- Each official match slot must specify an opponent spec:
  - `AiStyle` (technical / power / spin / balanced)
  - 3-skill profile: `edge`, `power`, `spin_inject` (obey skill budget cap from `STAGE2_LOCK.md`)
- If a rematch is meant to feel harder, that is authored by choosing a stronger opponent spec for that match.

0) **Onboarding Chain** (pre-hub, fixed)
- `S01` Early Arrival (Aya) → `M01` Aya Friendly (warm-up) → `S02` Club Intro (Aya → Coach) → `M02` Entry Benchmark (Benji) → `S03` Coach Brief (training unlock + Tix post-day) → `S04` YouTube → `M03` `1967` (dream) → `S05` Tix Midweek (choice) → optional `M04` Tix Lunch

1) **Week 1 — Placement (Anchor A)**
- Official: club placement match
- Intent: belonging test; establish initial “how the room reads you.”

2) **Week 2 — First Stakes**
- Official: club-scale match where result matters less than composure and effort.

3) **Week 3 — Style Becomes Visible**
- Official: first clear identity read (power / technical / spin / balanced).

4) **Week 4 — Trust + Friction**
- Official: minor-rival pressure beat (Benji lens); trust/ambiguity friction without melodrama.

5) **Week 5 — The Wall**
- Official: one fair, undeniable loss beat (no shame framing).

6) **Week 6 — Club Semifinal (Anchor B)**
- Official: club tournament semifinal; major-rival rematch (Jolo).

7) **Week 7 — Regional Pressure**
- Official: qualifier/setup match; group cohesion under pressure.

8) **Week 8 — Regional Decider (Anchor C)**
- Official: decider + quiet aftermath; continuity ending.

Implementation note:
- Weeks `2–5` and Week `7` are designed to be **content-light**: curated card packs keyed by tags + reputation, with only a few bespoke scenes required.

## Divergence Rules (Keep It Contained)
Season 1 divergence is **expression**, not branch explosion:
- Training blocks emit up to `2` training tags (see `STAGE2_TAGS.md`).
- Mainline matches emit up to `3` official tags (see `STAGE2_TAGS.md`) and update reputation state.
- Insert routing and speaker selection should be driven by:
  - commitment flags + training tags (`GRINDER` / `SLACKER`)
  - style tags (`TECHNICIAN` / `POWER_SPECIALIST` / `SPIN_SPECIALIST` / `RECKLESS`)
  - official tags (comeback/choke/upset/etc.)
  - relationship flags (Kai/Aya/Tix/Jolo/Benji/Issa)

### Effort × Performance “Social Color” (Guideline)
Use this as a default lens for tonal swaps; do not hard-branch structure.
- **High effort + high performance:** respect and pressure (Aya/Coach), rivalry sharpens (Jolo)
- **High effort + low performance:** concern and guidance (Kai/Aya/Coach), theory creep risk (Tix)
- **Low effort + high performance:** commitment dispute (Jolo/Coach), opportunism/social drift (Benji)
- **Low effort + low performance:** quiet isolation risk; Issa pulls you back to continuation
