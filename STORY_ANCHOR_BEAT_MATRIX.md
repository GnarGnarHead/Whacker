# Story Anchor Beat Matrix

## Scope
- Maps core cast roles across the three locked anchor beats.
- Planning artifact for narrative design before prose/node scripting.

## Anchors (Locked)
1. `Anchor A` - Club Entry Placement
2. `Anchor B` - Mid-Arc Conflict Resolution Match
3. `Anchor C` - Regional Decider + Quiet Aftermath

## Role Matrix

### Player Character
- `A`: outsider entering the club social map; first belonging test.
- `B`: conflict owner; must resolve tension through play.
- `C`: culmination of identity and maturity under pressure.

### Kai Alvarez
- `A`: entry bridge and first stable mirror.
- `B`: neutral witness who reads whether player adapts.
- `C`: closing mirror; confirms arc change without speeches.

### Aya Villanueva (balanced love interest, unresolved)
- `A`: first warm connection and emotional grounding.
- `B`: supportive counterweight; does not solve conflict for player.
- `C`: unresolved romantic thread lands quietly, no full payoff.

### Tino "Tix" Ramos (technical nerd; high IQ, low EQ)
- `A`: over-analytical first read of player, socially awkward delivery.
- `B`: strategy/philosophy friction vector (theory vs reality).
- `C`: tactical voice during pressure, can help or overtalk.

### Jolo Marasigan (power rival)
- `A`: immediate pressure check; status test energy.
- `B`: effort-seriousness conflict driver; challenges commitment.
- `C`: competitive benchmark for intensity and nerve.

### Benji Santos (low-empathy spin opportunist)
- `A`: socially polite but status-sensitive; probes the player with indirect comments and spin-heavy nuisance pressure.
- `B`: petty-opportunist flare point; uses deflection, selective honesty, and match-level misdirection instead of direct conflict.
- `C`: trajectory mirror for accountability; can either quietly own prior behavior or retreat into excuse-first defensiveness.

### Issa Flores (social butterfly)
- `A`: social glue that lowers initial friction.
- `B`: tries to mediate conflict; cannot remove need for match resolution.
- `C`: keeps team cohesion and emotional warmth post-match.

### Coach Reyes
- `A`: sets standards and role boundaries in few lines.
- `B`: frames conflict as table-solvable; authorizes resolution match.
- `C`: one-line framing in aftermath; grounded adult presence, no sermon.

## Anchor-Specific Variant Hooks

### Anchor A - Club Entry Placement
- `high_composure_intro`: Coach offers cautious respect; Benji shifts to low-friction testing tone.
- `chaotic_intro`: Tix/Jolo skepticism rises.
- `balanced_intro`: Aya/Kai affinity opens early.

### Anchor B - Mid-Arc Conflict Resolution Match
- `low_training`: Jolo-led accountability conflict.
- `high_training`: ambition/philosophy conflict (often with Tix).
- `style_mismatch`: disagreement on "right way" to play.
- Optional insert: `inserted_personal_match` to settle tension.

### Anchor C - Regional Decider + Quiet Aftermath
- `surge_path`: earned confidence tone.
- `fade_path`: pressure and doubt tone.
- `rebound_path`: resilience tone.
- Win/loss changes shade, not validity of ending.

## Interaction Design Rules
- Use these roles as behavioral constraints, not rigid scripts.
- Keep each anchor human-scale and school-scale.
- Preserve tone touchstones: restraint, no caricature villains, play-as-language.
