# Story Mode Principles (Locked)

## Canonical Role
- This is the canonical document for story-mode intent and non-negotiables.
- Other narrative docs should reference these rules instead of re-stating them.

## Core Identity
- `Whacker` is a short-form sports drama told through Pong + text.
- The campaign is about becoming a player identity, not farming wins.
- The story reacts to *how* you play and train, not just whether you win.

## Non-Negotiables
- No stat allocation menu.
- No currency, inventory, or RPG gear systems.
- Skill growth is usage-driven:
  - center usage -> power
  - edge usage -> technique/angle
  - paddle movement at contact -> spin
- Matches are honest:
  - no hidden adaptive difficulty to force wins
  - outcomes must remain mechanically credible
- Losing is not failure; it is a valid narrative branch.

## Campaign Loop Contract
- The campaign is convergent with divergent expression.
- Player actions in story mode are:
  1. `Next Match`
  2. `Training`
- `Training` is optional and repeatable.
- `Next Match` advances the authored mainline schedule.

## Narrative Philosophy
- Equal narrative weight across outcomes.
- Story tone changes with trajectory:
  - grinder vs slacker
  - rising vs falling reputation
  - style identity shifts over time
- The game should not shame low performance.
- The story should feel human and grounded, not tropey reward/punish rails.

## Feedback Style
- Use diegetic, in-world commentary for growth signals.
- Keep UI minimal and readable.
- Reinforce identity through short lines and style tags, not heavy exposition.

## Vertical Slice Done Criteria
- `New Career` and `Continue` work.
- Story hub loop is playable end-to-end.
- Training and mainline matches both feed progression.
- Skills grow from observed playstyle.
- Mainline results affect reputation and relationship state.
- Save/load preserves week, skills, and record.
- At least one post-match narrative feedback beat is shown every match.
