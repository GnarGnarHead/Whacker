## What They Care About (Prose)
Jolo cares about effort you can see. He trusts people who take hits and keep moving, and he mistrusts polished talk with no load-bearing action behind it. He wants to be the player who drives pace and makes others react.

He also cares, more than he admits, about not becoming a burden to his family. His appetite for risk is real, but so is his memory of what risk can cost in money, time, and worry.
