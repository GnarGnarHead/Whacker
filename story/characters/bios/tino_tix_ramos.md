## What They Care About (Prose)
Tix cares about fairness as implementation, not sentiment. He believes standards must be explicit and auditable, especially when rewards or opportunities are scarce. He trusts records, repeatable procedures, and specific language more than reputation or intention.

He also cares, quietly, about not failing people through negligence. He does not frame this as kindness and would likely reject that word. It appears instead as diligence: checking totals, preserving traceability, and refusing "close enough" when stakes are real.

## Public Self vs Private Self
- Public self: methodical, articulate, unembarrassed by rules, and frequently critical in tone
- Private pressure: fear that lenience today becomes unfairness tomorrow
- Internal contradiction: wants objective fairness for everyone, but often delivers it in ways that isolate both himself and others
