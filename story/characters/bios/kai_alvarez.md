## What They Care About (Prose)
Kai cares about games staying playable for everyone in the room. He pays attention to turn order, access, and whether somebody is being edged out by noise or status. He does not frame this as virtue; to him, it is a practical condition for a real game.

He also cares about reliability under pressure. He prefers decisions that can be repeated and defended later, even when they are less glamorous in the moment. He wants to win, but not by pretending process does not matter.
