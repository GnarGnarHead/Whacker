## What They Care About (Prose)
Benji cares about access: better tools, better court time, and better positioning in rooms where scarcity is normal. He is attentive to procedure, but mostly as a terrain to navigate rather than a value to protect. He notices who adults trust and adapts his behavior to match that image.

He also cares about not being publicly exposed as weak or replaceable. This gives him a defensive opportunism. He does not usually seek big harm; he seeks small advantages that accumulate and are hard to pin down.
