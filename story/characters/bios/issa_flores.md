## What They Care About (Prose)
Issa cares about whether people can continue, not whether moments look dramatic. She tracks small constraints that most people miss: wet shoes, missed meals, late transport, emotional overload. Her care shows up as structure, not speeches.

She also cares about group durability. She sees teams as systems that fail at the edges first, and she works those edges by instinct. This makes her appear effortless, though the effort is constant and often private.
